Job Portal & Recruitment Dashboard

Job seekers sign up, get an ATS score for their CV, apply and follow every status change, while recruiters rank applicants by ATS score and job match inside Odoo.

💡 Built on standard Odoo Recruitment
Every application is a normal Recruitment applicant linked to the candidate’s contact, so your pipeline, stages, email templates, interviews and reports keep working exactly as before. Access is automatic: portal users see the Candidate Dashboard, users with the Recruitment Officer or Manager right get Recruitment › Dashboard inside Odoo, and website visitors who are not signed in see no dashboard menu.
RECRUITMENT › DASHBOARD · INSIDE ODOO
CANDIDATE DASHBOARD · /my/jobs

How it works

1
Recruiter publishes a job
Recruitment officers open the Recruitment app, which now lands on its Dashboard, create a position and switch it on for the website from the job table.
2
Candidate applies
Job seekers sign up for free, land on /my/jobs, bookmark positions and apply with their saved CV. The applicant appears in the job’s pipeline instantly.
3
Both sides stay in sync
Recruiters move applicants through stages, refuse or schedule interviews; the candidate sees each change as a portal notification and on their calendar.

Key features

📊Candidate dashboard
Applied, saved, shortlisted and unread-notification counters, recent applications, upcoming interviews and recommended jobs on one screen.
✨Recommended jobs
Published positions ranked against the candidate’s desired position and key skills, excluding jobs they already applied to.
🔍Browse & save jobs
Search by title, department, city or country, open a full job page inside the portal and bookmark jobs to apply later.
⚡One-step application
Applies with the profile CV or a new upload (PDF, Word, ODT, RTF, TXT — 10 MB), blocks duplicate applications and logs the source.
✅Applied jobs tracking
All, in-progress, shortlisted and closed tabs with a candidate-friendly status for each application.
🎥Interviews & calendar
Meetings scheduled from the applicant appear with time, location, recruiter and a Join button for online calls, plus a monthly calendar.
🔔Portal notifications
Automatic messages when an application is submitted, moves stage, is shortlisted, hired or not selected, and when an interview is set or rescheduled.
💼Recruitment dashboard in Odoo
Opens when recruiters click the Recruitment app: published jobs, applications in progress, new this week, upcoming interviews and hired this month.
📝Job positions table
Every position with recruiter, a website publish switch, pipeline size, new applicants and hired vs. target — filter and search instantly.
🗂Pipeline by stage
A bar per recruitment stage across all positions, plus the share of applications that came through the candidate portal.
📋Opens native Odoo views
Positions open the standard applicant kanban, applicants and jobs open their normal forms, interviews open the calendar — with breadcrumbs back to the dashboard.
👋Welcome popup & highlighted menu
Signed-in candidates get a welcome card once per visit with their applications, shortlist, updates and next interview, and a highlighted Candidate Dashboard menu button.
📈ATS CV score & job match
Every CV is scored out of 100 against ATS-compliant résumé rules (format, contact details, standard sections, layout, fonts, length, dates, bullets, action verbs, results) plus a keyword match % for the job.
👤Candidate profile
Phone, LinkedIn, desired position, experience, skills, preferred location and CV, with a verification step and a reminder banner until it is complete.
⚙️ Configuration
  1. Install the module (it depends on Website Recruitment). Free sign-up is switched on for your websites and a Candidate Dashboard menu is added.
  2. Publish job positions on the website as usual (Recruitment › Job Positions › Is Published).
  3. Under Recruitment › Configuration › Stages, tick Shortlisted in Candidate Portal on the stages that mean “shortlisted” and optionally set a Candidate-facing Label to hide internal stage names. Interview and contract stages are pre-ticked.
  4. In Recruitment › Settings, keep Candidate Dashboard on so job seekers opening /my land directly on /my/jobs.
  5. Give recruiters the Recruitment › Officer or Administrator right. The Recruitment app then opens on Dashboard; interviewers without that right keep the standard landing page.
  6. Schedule interviews from the recruiter dashboard or the applicant’s Meetings button — the candidate is notified and sees it on their dashboard.
⚠️ Good to know
One application per candidate and job, and no applications once a position’s Target is reached (it shows a “Position Filled” ribbon; recruiters cannot hire beyond the target either). Candidates only see applications linked to their own contact — never matched by email, because sign-up does not prove email ownership. Portal users get no direct access to the new models; the Recruitment dashboard runs with each recruiter’s own access rights, so record rules and multi-company settings still apply. Depends on: website_hr_recruitment, portal, auth_signup, calendar.
